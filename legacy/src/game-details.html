How to Dunk

Full guide →

Every dunk input in NBA 2K27, for Xbox and PlayStation, plus how the dunk meter works and how to time the release. 4 moves.

MoveInput (Xbox)
Normal DunkRT + press X while driving in close range.
Two-Hand DunkRT + move and hold RS up while driving in close range.
Dominant or Off-Hand DunkRT + move and hold RS left or right. The RS direction determines the dunking hand.
Controllable Rim HangWhen performing a dunk with RS, keep RT held to hang on the rim. Use LS to control swing, and move RS up to pull up on the rim or left/right to lift the legs.

How to Pass

Full guide →

18 moves.

MoveInput (Xbox)
Normal PassPress A to pass. Use LS to select receiver.
Bounce PassTap B to pass. Use LS to select receiver.
Lob PassPress Y to pass. Use LS to select receiver.
Icon PassPress RB then press the button of the desired receiver. Use LS to lead the receiver in any direction.
Wrap PassDouble tap B to pass. Use LS to select receiver.
Alley-oopDouble tap Y to pass. Use LS to select receiver. To finish the alley-oop, press the button displayed once the meter reaches the make window when receiving the pass.
Alley-oop To SelfPress X + Y + move LS toward the hoop. To finish the alley-oop, press the button displayed once the meter reaches the make window when receiving the pass.
Lead To Basket PassPress and hold Y to make the selected receiver cut to the basket. Release Y to pass. (Lead To Basket Pass must be enabled in Controller Settings.)
Skip PassHold A to target a receiver farther away. Use LS to select receiver.
Get Open PassHold B to call for a teammate to get open. Release B to pass the ball. Use LS to select receiver.
Handoff PassHold B to call for a teammate to come to the ball for a handoff. Use LS to select receiver. (Handoff/Pitch Pass must be enabled in Controller Settings.)
Fake PassPress Y + B while standing or driving to the hoop.
Jump PassPress X + A while standing or driving to the hoop.
Flashy PassWhile holding RB, move RS towards a teammate.
Full Receiver ControlPress and hold B to freely move the selected receiver with LS. Release B to pass. (Full Receiver Control must be enabled in Controller Settings.)
Touch PassPress A before the initial receiver gets the ball. Use LS to select second receiver.
Give and GoPress and hold A until the receiver catches the ball. While keeping A held, use LS to move the initial passer, then release A to get the ball back.
Rolling InboundPress Y during baseline inbounds when no defenders are present.

How to Layups

Full guide →

9 moves.

MoveInput (Xbox)
Quick Scoop LayupMove and hold RS left or right while driving.
Step ThroughPress and hold RS in any direction to perform a step through after you've lost your dribble. Attempts from far out will result in shots, while trying this near the basket will result in a layup.
Normal LayupPress X while driving in close range. OR Move and hold RS up while driving in close range.
Euro Step LayupMove RS down left while driving. OR Double tap X while driving.
Spin LayupHold RT and double tap X while driving, then release RT during the animation.
Slow Step DecelWhile performing a euro-step layup, press and hold LT to slow down your gather and try to throw the defense off-balance.
Runner / FloaterMove and hold RS down while driving in close range.
Reverse LayupMove and hold RS right while driving along the right baseline.
Hop Step FinishRT + move RS down left or right while driving. OR Tap X while driving.

How to Post

Full guide →

21 moves.

MoveInput (Xbox)
Post Go-To ShotHold LT to post up. With LS neutral, move and hold RS up.
Post HookHold LT to post up. With LS neutral, move and hold RS up left or up right.
Post FadeHold LT to post up. Move and hold RS left or right away from the hoop.
Post Shimmy HookHold LT to post up. Hold RT + move and hold RS up left or up right.
Post Shimmy FadeHold LT to post up. Hold RT + move and hold RS left or right.
Post Up and UnderHold LT to post up. Use RS to pump fake, then let go of LT and quickly move and hold RS again before the pump fake ends.
Post LayupHold LT to post up. Move LS toward the hoop + hold RS up left or up right.
Post Dunk AttemptHold LT to post up. Hold RT and move LS + RS up.
Post Hop ShotHold LT to post up. Move and hold LS left, right, or down then tap X. OR Hold RT + move and hold RS away from the hoop.
Post Pump FakeHold LT to post up. Move RS in any direction then quickly release (while holding the ball).
Enter and Leave PostPress and hold LT to post up. Release LT to leave the post and faceup.
Post PivotPress and hold LT to post up. Move LS in any direction then quickly release (while holding the ball).
Drop StepHold LT to post up. Move and hold LS left or right toward the hoop then tap X.
Spin or DriveHold LT to post up. Then, rotate RS to either shoulder.
Switch FacingHold LT to post up. Move RS up then quickly release.
Drive FakeHold LT to post up. Hold RT and move RS towards the inside shoulder then quickly release.
Spin FakeHold LT to post up. Hold RT and move RS towards the outside shoulder then quickly release.
Post Step-AwayHold LT to post up. Hold RT and move RS down then quickly release.
Disengage AwayHold LT to post up. Then, release LT while moving and holding the LS away from the basket.
Aggressive BackdownHold LT to post up. Hold RT + LS toward the defender.
Avoid StealHold LT to post up. Move RS down and quickly release.
